Habit Heist RPG

A gamified task tracker where completing daily habits earns experience points to upgrade a digital character and unlock randomized loot drops.

Level Up Your Reality

Turn your mundane daily to-do list into a high-stakes adventure. Every habit you complete grants you experience points, fueling your character's journey from a humble squire to a legendary hero.

The Loot Vault

Consistency is the ultimate weapon in Habit Heist. Maintain your streaks to trigger procedural loot drops, rewarding your discipline with rare digital gear and unique aesthetic upgrades for your avatar.

Persistent Progression

Your progress is locked in locally, ensuring your hard-earned stats and inventory remain safe. Track your growth through intuitive dashboards that visualize your productivity as a growing power level.
